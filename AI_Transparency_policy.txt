# Use of Artificial Intelligence
** This website does not utilise Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), or automated decision-making systems to process any data, or interact with users. It does however utilise third party artificial intelligence (AI) tools to create graphics for some of our projects, translate content from English to Greek, and Greek to English. These third party tools are hosted in sites other than our own, and and are not integrated any any way with our sites.
# Human Oversight and Decision Making
** While AI assists in our operations named above, all decisions are subject to human review. We do not engage in solely automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects.
# Data Processing and Input
** Our sites do not have any AI features.
# Accuracy and Limitations
** AI-generated content is based on patterns in data and may occasionally produce inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information and it should not be relied upon. In the case of our translated texts to Greek, verify critical information by reading the original texts in English or Greek. Our translated pages indicate the original language of the text.
# Model Information
** We use various AI models found through the net but none are embedded or installed in our websites.
