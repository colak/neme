France
The minimum remuneration is €100 for any artist whose work is presented in the framework of a group exhibition, whatever its duration.
If the exhibition presents less than 10 artists, a total amount of €1,000 per exhibition must be divided by the number of artists (€500 per artist for a two person exhibition, €250 for an exhibition of four artists…).
If the exhibition presents more than 10 artists, a minimum remuneration of €100 per artist is applied.
Annual turnover: €68,000
Fee: €100
Average salary in France: €2,277
www.culture.gouv.fr/Thematiques/Arts-plastiques/Actualites/La-remuneration-du-droit-de-presentation-publique
